# Demo films

Short product films in the Movate style: a hook, sourced stats, numbered steps over live footage of the Kirby Operations Hub, outcomes, end card.

- `Kirby_NOC_Network_Agent.mp4`: NOC film 1, an Airtel MPLS degradation across five India sales offices handled end to end.

Rebuild (from `films/src`, with `index.html` copied in as `hub.html` and `npm i @fontsource/manrope`):

    python3 music.py bed.wav 140 3 31
    node rec.js noc1.js frames
    python3 assemble.py frames out.mp4 bed.wav

Stats: ITIC 2024 Hourly Cost of Downtime Survey; Uptime Institute Annual Outage Analysis 2025. Music is generated by `music.py`.
